Hello and welcome to my website. My name is Mike Simpson, and I'm a Research Software Engineer and former Game Developer, currently based in the North-East of England.

I am also:

  • Trustee & President of the Society of Research Software Engineering (2023-present).
  • 2025 Institute for Research Software Fellow.

On this site, you will find information about my eclectic collection of skills, hobbies and interests, including my research software and gaming portfolios, as well as a blog about a range of topics, including writing, photography, gaming and mental health.

Background

I have a degree in Computer Science and an MSc in Computer Games Engineering. I worked as a games programmer before returning to university to complete a PhD. I now work as a Research Software Engineer at Newcastle University.

  • You can learn more about my background on the About page.

Research Software Engineering

As a Research Software Engineer, I have worked on dozens of research projects in a number of different fields, enabling their research through my experience with software and website development, data visualisation and general software engineering best practices.

  • More on the projects and publications I have contributed to on the Research page.

Writing

I'm a lifelong fan of sci-fi and fantasy, and an aspiring writer. I believe that an ability to tell engaging stories is a vital skill when it comes to science communication. I enjoy writing fiction, as well as blog posts about a range of topics, including mental health.

  • Some examples of my writing can be found on the Writing page.

Video Game Development

I also took an industrial placement project at Midway Studios Newcastle during my MSc. Then, as a Game Programmer, I worked at a local studio for a number of years, contributing to titles including NASCAR the Game 2011 and Auto Club Revolution.

  • For more information about some of these projects, check out my Game Portfolio.

3D Graphics

I learned to use Blender, the open-source 3D creation suite, for a series of projects involving interactive 3d data visualisations. I have also been developing my 3D modelling skills in my spare time via a range of creative projects.

  • Check out some of my creations on my Artwork page.

Photography

I am also a keen photographer who has worked professionally at a number of events. I'm currently working on improving my skills and building up a portfolio, though for now, I am treating it as more of a hobby.

  • More examples and information can be found on my Photography page.
